## Work log

- **Exhibition opens today.** No code goes near the machine; the day is plans and pipeline.

- Applied the architecture review to the four chapter plans (`48c1c5f`). The important ones:
  hotspots are the document's (the Futures plan had invented `pins.ts` beside a `Hotspot` type
  that already had `LocaleMap`), `ChapterId` exists because the rail was keyed by view and
  breaks the moment Futures enters the valley, and `registers.ts` is dismantled rather than
  renamed — `Diorama` still imported six things from it. Sized the column budget: 512² is
  534k triangles, 3.56× the phone ceiling, and the exhibition screen draws it anyway, which
  is now a recorded priority in `CLAUDE.md`.

- **Copy comes from a Google Doc.** Yan's decision; the doc exists with four empty tabs. The
  reference script in `malaysia-data-center-story` goes public-export → Turndown → ArchieML.
  Two things didn't carry: the doc is private (export URL 401s) and that script isn't
  tab-aware. `gws` is upstream of both problems — the Docs API hands over `link.url` per run
  and per-tab content, so links are preserved rather than reconstructed. `docToMarkdown` and
  `renderCopy` are pure and tested; the script is transport only; no credential in the repo.
  Byte-identical on re-run, proven. Bold and italic are dropped on purpose — a bold `key:`
  becomes `**key:**` and ArchieML reads prose. That is the exact bug the reference script
  regexes around.

- **The shell is scrollable.** Five foundations, five commits: `ChapterId` (and it fixed the
  number keys, which were still on the old scale order), the `Hotspot` extension with
  `validateCopyJoin`, `camera.ts` extended plus `CameraRig` — and `registers.ts` finally
  deleted, three days after CLAUDE.md said it had been; its crossfade watcher was defined and
  never mounted — `chapters.ts` with `beatAt`/`resolvePose`/`validateScore`, and then the
  visible layer: the viewer is the scroll container, the stage sticks, one section per beat
  scrolls over it with a centred card, the beat names the view and a pose relative to its fit,
  and the terminal beat flips `mode` to explore. Scores are committed content joined to the
  doc's copy by id, with a test that fails the build if either side drifts. Nobody has looked
  at it: Chrome will not screenshot `/`. What I could check — the server-rendered HTML carries
  the stem, the lit first card, the sample headline and the hyperlink as a real anchor; the
  console is clean across two loads; both routes build.

- **Seeded the doc.** Yan asked for sample copy in the Google Doc, so the four tabs now hold
  12 beats and 16 hotspots written from the research notes, with the ids the chapter context
  files use — the join to coordinates and icons is already honest. One real hyperlink per
  chapter, deliberately, so the round trip is demonstrable rather than asserted: all four came
  back as `[text](url)` inside parsed ArchieML values. Written with `gws docs documents
  batchUpdate`, one `insertText` per tab and an `updateTextStyle` with `link` per phrase,
  offsets computed from the segments. Byte-identical on re-run with real content.

- Wrote the test "never emits HTML" wrong the first time. Runs are data; `<script>` in copy is
  legitimate text that React escapes. The guarantee is the run's *shape* — `text` and an
  optional safe `href` — and now the test says so.

  **The camera: a wrong turn, and what it left behind.** I read "keep the orientation
  north to south" as north-up, built it — camera due south, 45° pitch, backdrop
  re-rendered, 583 tests — and Yan saw it and said no: "we want the same angle we had
  before". The angle is back on the diagonal. What survives is that the attitude now
  lives in ONE place, `camera.ts` (`CAMERA_YAW`, `CAMERA_PITCH`, `screenBasis`,
  `projectView`, `groundDepth`, `wallFacesCamera`, `rightness`), where before it was
  restated in six files; the backdrop fingerprint hashes it, so turning the camera fails
  `backdrop-freshness.test.ts` until `npm run render:backdrop`; and the circle's fit is
  computed from its own bounds rather than derived from the district's. The committed
  raster changed by a few bytes — a wall within 15° of the tone split now takes the lit
  tone — and the sidecar carries the new fingerprint.

  **What "orientation" meant is still open.** Yan's screenshot of the valley this morning
  shows a wide slab with its top edge tilted about 7°, which is neither the diagonal's
  rhombus nor anything the square 120 km field can produce at that camera. Chrome froze
  before I could measure the canvas in the stem; my one stem screenshot had a blank stage
  where the valley should have been. Browser verification is Yan's from here, by request.

- **Present, started** (`plans/2026-09-23_present-chapter`, now `active`). The three region
  artefacts regenerated on the scene origin with `build-region.py --centre 18.7912,99.0043`;
  the generator now writes a `curve` into each sidecar — cumulative population every 50 km —
  and `region.ts` reads it with `peopleWithin` and `halfPopulationRadius`. The new 3,437 km
  field holds 3.98 bn against 4.09 bn at the Valeriepieris centre: the move cost 2.6%, and the
  curve matches the plan's table (2,000 km → 1.61 bn, 3,000 km → 3.60 bn). `region.test.ts`
  fails if a field's centre differs from the scene origin, which is the test that catches a
  regeneration on the old centre.

  The field is columns now: `columns.ts` (pure — layout, a three-face unit box derived from
  the camera basis, log-scaled height) and `RegionColumns.tsx` (one `InstancedMesh`, ring
  radius as a uniform patched into `MeshBasicMaterial`, picking by instance id). A beat can
  carry `ring: { from, to }` in multiples of the claim radius; `ringAt` interpolates it with
  the beat's progress, and a ring beat also interpolates the camera toward the next beat's
  pose, so the world recedes as the circle grows. The counter under the card reads the curve.
  The explore caption says "about 3,400 km", rounded to the hundred, with the 7.8–8.2 bn
  bracket as the footnote — the plan's three open questions taken as: rounded with a footnote,
  the ring keeps growing past the claim in the muted tone, and the field's own 3,437 km rim
  stays drawn as the ghost of the old radius.
