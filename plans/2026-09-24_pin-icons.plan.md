# Pin icons — sixteen isometric landmarks, generated on Higgsfield

## Context

Both the Past and the Futures chapters stand oversized icons on the landscape: three point items
in the Past (the station, the Khun Tan tunnel, the airport), the twelve Futures places, and Wat
Ket itself for the Present. The Past plan says the two chapters share one icon system, and the
Futures context says why the icons are isometric sprites rather than models: the camera is
orthographic and never rotates, so a sprite and a `.glb` are indistinguishable. Production was
always going to be generated artwork iterated through Higgsfield with a jig to compare candidates
(`2026-09-23_futures-chapter`, `2026-09-24_scrolly-shell`).

This started on 24 Sep 2026 while the Present chapter was still being built — Yan asked for it to
run in parallel. The subjects come from the revised exhibition copy at
`docs/references/wat-ket-exhibition-copy-gpt.md` (local only) and the futures context file. The
sixteen ids are the hotspot ids, so the join to copy and coordinates is already honest.

**The look is the key visual's.** `docs/references/kv ASSET/` holds the programme's own artwork:
paper-cut and felt relief objects — chedi, palms, a pavilion, a bridge — in the purple family
with cream and gold, on white. Yan's instruction, 24 Sep: that style is right, but the icons have
to be isometric for the map.

## Goal

Sixteen distinct isometric icons in the KV's material language and the theme's palette, one per
hotspot id, committed as optimised assets under `public/icons/`, each one readable as a landmark
at the size it will be drawn on the diorama and the valley. A future session can regenerate or
add one by editing a manifest entry and running one script.

## Approach

**A style probe before the set.** One subject (KAE at Wiang Kum Kam) on three models with the
same prompt: Nano Banana 2 with three KV images as references, Recraft V4.1 with the palette
pinned, GPT Image 2 with references and a transparent background. Result, 24 Sep 09:36: all three
came back isometric; Nano Banana held the palette and the felt texture and read as one landmark;
Recraft went flat vector and lost the relief; GPT Image 2 was the richest and broke the palette
with real terracotta brick, at three times the cost. **Nano Banana 2 with references is the
production model, at 2 credits an image.**

**One style block, sixteen subjects.** `scripts/gen-icons.py` holds the style text — the camera
stated as the renderer's own figures (45° plan rotation, 35° pitch, from `camera.ts`), the
palette by hex, the KV material, and the negatives — and `scripts/icons/manifest.json` holds only
what differs per icon. The set cannot drift icon by icon because nothing per-icon can touch the
style. The one fix carried from the probe: every model put the object on a heavy ground tile,
which would sit on top of the map, so the block asks for the object directly on white with no
plinth.

**Candidates are scratch; chosen icons are assets.** `data/icons/` is gitignored — the probe, the
KV refs copied in, and `candidates/<id>/<round>-<n>.png` with its prompt and job JSON beside it.
The script writes a labelled contact sheet at `data/icons/contact-sheet.png`, which is the jig for
now: Yan looks, names the keepers or asks for another round on some ids, and a keeper is copied
to `public/icons/<id>.png` after background removal and downscaling. The `/jig` page in the shell
plan is still wanted, but a PNG contact sheet gets the first round in front of Yan today.

**Budget.** The account had 126 credits at the start. Probe: ~10. Round one, sixteen icons at one
candidate each: 32. That leaves roughly 84 for second rounds — about two more full passes, or
targeted rerolls on the ones that miss.

**Rejected: modelling, or 3D generation.** Nothing to photograph, no orbit to justify a mesh, and
the backdrop raster already forbids orbit. See the futures context.

**Rejected: vector.** Recraft's vector mode would give crisp scaling for free, but the KV is a
relief texture and the vector output loses exactly that. Sharpness comes from generating at 2k and
downscaling.

## Verification

- Open `data/icons/contact-sheet.png`: sixteen rows, each an isometric object on white, no plinth,
  palette held, no text in the image. Each reads as a different place at thumbnail size.
- `data/icons/keyed-on-purple.png`: the sixteen keyed sprites composited on Cosmo Purple, so a
  white fringe or a hole in a cream wall would show.
- Once in `public/icons/`: a hotspot with `icon: 'kae'` draws the sprite at the pin's position at
  both scales, and it is legible at the projector's district fit.

## Out of scope

- Pin rendering, hover, popup and story overlay — the shell plan.
- Placing the hotspots in `wat-ket.json` — the chapter plans.
- Thai captions, or any text inside an icon. Labels are DOM.
- The `/jig` page. A contact sheet does the job for round one; build the page when rounds get
  frequent enough to want it.
